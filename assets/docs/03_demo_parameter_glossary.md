# Synthetic Parameter Glossary

This glossary supports tool demonstrations only. Names and mappings are examples, not a vehicle standard.

| Canonical demo label | Example aliases | Demo unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combined range | Range, combined driving range | km |
| Battery energy | Battery pack energy, rated battery energy | kWh |
| Nominal voltage | Rated voltage, operating voltage | V |
| Peak motor output | Maximum motor power | kW |
| Curb mass | Vehicle mass | kg |
| Wheelbase | Axle distance | mm |

Direct conversions implemented for the demo include Wh/kWh, h/min, and kV/V. A capacity in mAh cannot be converted to kWh without voltage and should be manually reviewed. Similar labels do not prove that two fields have the same regulatory definition.
